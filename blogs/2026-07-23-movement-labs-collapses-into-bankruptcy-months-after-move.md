MVMT Labs, Inc. filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy on 15 July 2026 in a Delaware court, listing under $500,000 in assets against liabilities of up to $10 million. The filing follows the 2024 MOVE token scandal and the departure of co-founder Rushi Manche.
