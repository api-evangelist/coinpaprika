Goldman Sachs has made its roughly $100 billion FTIXX Treasury fund available to institutional crypto firms through Lynq, a settlement network, without tokenizing it. Trades run through SEC-registered broker-dealer tZERO Securities, and FTIXX is the first outside fund on the network.
