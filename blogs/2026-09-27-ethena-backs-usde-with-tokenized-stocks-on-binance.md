Ethena has started backing its USDe synthetic dollar with tokenized U.S. stocks on Binance, extending its delta-neutral basis trade beyond crypto for the first time. Allocations began on 25 September 2026, using Binance bStocks as collateral hedged by equity perpetual futures.
