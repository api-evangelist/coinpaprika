SoFi and Payward, the parent company of crypto exchange Kraken, agreed to connect their financial infrastructure. Kraken joins SoFi's around-the-clock dollar settlement network and lists the SoFiUSD stablecoin, while SoFi uses Kraken Prime for crypto trade liquidity.
