Anchorpoint began the phased rollout of its Hong Kong dollar-backed stablecoin HKDAP on 12 August 2026, opening beta access to institutional distributors and professional investors. The Standard Chartered-backed issuer said its first use cases are cross-border payments and settlement of tokenised real-world assets.
