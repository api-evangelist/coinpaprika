Wyoming has adopted Chainlink Proof of Reserve to verify the reserves backing its Frontier Stable Token (FRNT), the first stable token issued by a U.S. state. It delivers reserve data onchain in near real time, exceeding the monthly disclosure required under the federal GENIUS Act.
