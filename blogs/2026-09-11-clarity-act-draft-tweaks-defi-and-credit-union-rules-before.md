Senate Republicans circulated a revised Digital Asset Market Clarity Act on 10 September 2026, adding rules for some decentralized finance (DeFi) protocols and clarifying how credit unions handle crypto. The bill faces a 60-vote cloture test on 15 September, and its passage remains uncertain.
