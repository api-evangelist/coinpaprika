Ripple Prime, the non-bank prime brokerage owned by Ripple, closed an upsized $275 million private placement of senior unsecured notes on 18 August 2026. Credit agency KBRA rated the notes investment-grade BBB, and Piper Sandler acted as lead placement agent.
