Ripple is targeting corporate treasury clients that handle about $13 trillion in transactions each year to expand its RLUSD stablecoin. The company's stablecoin chief, Jack McDonald, said its Ripple Treasury platform serves roughly 1,200 corporate treasurers and CFOs.
