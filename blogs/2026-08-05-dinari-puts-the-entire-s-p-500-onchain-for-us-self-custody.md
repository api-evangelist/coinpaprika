Dinari launched 724 tokenized U.S. stocks on 4 August 2026, covering the entire S&P 500, for eligible investors to trade through self-custody wallets. The tokens, called dShares, use Circle's USDC stablecoin and are backed one-for-one by shares held in regulated custody.
