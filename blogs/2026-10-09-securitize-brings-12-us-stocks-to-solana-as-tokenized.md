Securitize launched tokenized versions of 12 major US stocks on the Solana blockchain on 8 October 2026, each backed one-to-one by real shares held at its regulated broker-dealer. Holders keep dividends, voting rights, and corporate-action entitlements, with trades settling in the USDC stablecoin.
