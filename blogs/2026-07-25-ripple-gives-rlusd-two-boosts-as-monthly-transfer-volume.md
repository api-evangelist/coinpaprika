Ripple launched Ripple Mint, an automated platform for institutions to mint, redeem and bridge its RLUSD stablecoin, and invested in compliance firm Notabene. The moves came as RLUSD monthly transfer volume fell about 25%, from roughly $14.6 billion to $11 billion.
