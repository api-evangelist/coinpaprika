Bitcoin treasury companies including Satsuma Technology and Strategy are selling holdings, repaying debt and pivoting to artificial intelligence after Bitcoin fell about 49% from its October 2025 peak. Satsuma shareholders approved liquidating all 668 BTC and delisting from the London Stock Exchange.
