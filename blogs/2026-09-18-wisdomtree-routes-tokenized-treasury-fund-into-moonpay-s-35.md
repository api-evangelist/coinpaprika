WisdomTree and MoonPay announced a collaboration on 17 September 2026 to widen U.S. access to the tokenized WisdomTree Treasury Money Market Digital Fund. MoonPay plans to use the fund within its stablecoin reserve management across a network of over 35 million accounts.
