Germany's financial regulator BaFin refused a Markets in Crypto-Assets licence to futurum bank AG, the Bitcoin Group SE subsidiary that operates Bitcoin.de. The platform, which serves more than 1.1 million users, has had trading suspended since June 12, 2026.
