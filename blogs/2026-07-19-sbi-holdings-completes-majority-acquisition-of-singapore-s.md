SBI Holdings has completed a majority-stake acquisition of Singapore crypto exchange Coinhako after clearance from the Monetary Authority of Singapore. The deal, made through subsidiary SBI Ventures Asset, makes Coinhako a consolidated subsidiary of the Japanese financial group.
