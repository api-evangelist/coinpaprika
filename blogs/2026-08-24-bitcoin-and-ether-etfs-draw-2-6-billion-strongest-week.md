U.S. spot bitcoin and ether exchange-traded funds drew a combined $2.6 billion in net inflows over the five trading sessions from 17 to 21 August 2026, their strongest week since October 2025. Bitcoin funds took $1.9 billion and ether funds $697.2 million, both 2026 records.
