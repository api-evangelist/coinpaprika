The US Treasury sanctioned two Iranian-linked crypto exchanges, Shelbit and Aban Tether, on 7 August 2026 for helping move funds for the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps. The action added both platforms to the US sanctions blocklist under the 'Economic Fury' campaign.
