Thailand's Securities and Exchange Commission finalized rules letting Bitcoin and Ether exchange-traded funds list on the Stock Exchange of Thailand from October 16, 2026. Initial offerings cover only Bitcoin and Ether and must hold at least 80% net exposure to a single asset.
