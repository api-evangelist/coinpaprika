Tokenized stock markets built on Hyperliquid's HIP-3 framework now account for close to 50% of the exchange's perpetuals volume, up from about 2% in January. TradeXYZ leads the category, running Nasdaq-100 and single-stock perpetual contracts settled in stablecoin.
