Coinbase filed with the U.S. Commodity Futures Trading Commission on 18 September 2026 to list single-stock perpetual futures for American traders. The plan covers roughly 50 to 60 contracts, including Apple, Microsoft, Tesla and Nvidia, pending regulatory approval.
