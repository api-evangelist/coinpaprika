The U.S. Department of Commerce and Chainlink brought six official economic data feeds onchain, covering Real GDP, the PCE Price Index, and Real Final Sales across ten blockchains. The feeds delivered the advance second-quarter 2026 GDP estimate, which showed 1.5% annualized growth.
