Securitize has launched the Neuberger Securitize High Income Tokenized Fund, or HINC, across Avalanche, Ethereum, Solana, and Sui. The fund marks Neuberger's first role as subadvisor to a tokenized fund, drawing on a fixed-income platform overseeing more than $230 billion.
