Raydium on Solana and Uniswap on Ethereum each launched KYC-gated trading pools on 23 July 2026, with tokenization firm Superstate as the first compliance partner on both chains. The pools enforce investor eligibility at the protocol level, letting regulated assets trade on decentralized exchanges.
