Circle launched Digital Asset-Backed Borrowing on 21 September 2026, letting eligible Circle Mint institutions borrow USDC against Bitcoin without selling it. Customers deposit BTC, mint the wrapped token cirBTC, and supply it as collateral on Morpho markets across Arc and Ethereum.
