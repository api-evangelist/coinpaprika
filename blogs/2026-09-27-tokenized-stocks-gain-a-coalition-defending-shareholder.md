Bullish, Equiniti, Alpaca, Apex Fintech Solutions and DriveWealth formed the Issuer Sponsored Token Coalition on 24 September 2026. The group plans to set standards linking tokenized stocks to official shareholder registers, preserving voting rights and dividends.
