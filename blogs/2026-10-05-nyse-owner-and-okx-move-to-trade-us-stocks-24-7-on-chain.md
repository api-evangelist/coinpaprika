OKXICE, a 50-50 joint venture between OKX and New York Stock Exchange parent Intercontinental Exchange, has notified the SEC of plans for a tokenized U.S. stock venue. The platform would offer round-the-clock trading of shares in more than 60 U.S.-listed companies.
