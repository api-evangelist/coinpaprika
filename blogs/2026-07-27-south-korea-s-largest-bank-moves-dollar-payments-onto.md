KB Kookmin Bank, South Korea's largest lender, will launch a corporate cross-border payment service in August 2026 on JPMorgan's Kinexys blockchain. The service initially supports US dollar transfers to businesses across 10 countries and connects to the SWIFT network for near real-time settlement.
