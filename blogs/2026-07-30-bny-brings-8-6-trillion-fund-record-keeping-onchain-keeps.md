Bank of New York Mellon is moving its transfer agency business, which services $8.6 trillion across 7.6 million accounts, onto blockchain. The system creates a single on-chain ownership ledger for tokenized funds, with Baillie Gifford, BlackRock and its Dreyfus unit as early clients.
