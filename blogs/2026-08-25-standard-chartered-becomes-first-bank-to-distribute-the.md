Standard Chartered has become the first bank to distribute HKDAP, the Hong Kong dollar-backed stablecoin issued by Anchorpoint Financial. The bank, Anchorpoint's majority shareholder, will help institutional clients integrate the token for fund subscriptions, settlement and cross-border payments.
