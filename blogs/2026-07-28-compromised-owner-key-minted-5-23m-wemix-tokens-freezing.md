A compromised contract owner minted 5.23 million unauthorised WEMIX$ tokens on 26 July 2026, breaking the stablecoin's minting controls. WEMIX froze its bridges, liquidity pools, and DEX after the attacker moved roughly $724,000 across Ethereum and BNB Smart Chain.
