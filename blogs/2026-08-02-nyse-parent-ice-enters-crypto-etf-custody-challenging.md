Intercontinental Exchange, the parent of the New York Stock Exchange, is pitching its ICE Digital Trust unit as a custody partner for crypto exchange-traded funds. The move targets a digital-asset ETF market holding roughly $184 billion, where Coinbase secures over 80% of US assets.
