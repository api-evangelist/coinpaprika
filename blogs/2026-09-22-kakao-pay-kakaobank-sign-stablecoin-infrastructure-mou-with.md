Kakao Pay and KakaoBank signed a non-binding memorandum with crypto infrastructure firm Fireblocks on 21 September 2026 to explore stablecoin and digital asset services in South Korea. The agreement covers proof-of-concept tests tailored to the country's regulatory and security rules.
