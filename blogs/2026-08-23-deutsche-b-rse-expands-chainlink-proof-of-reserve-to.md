Deutsche Börse's Crypto Finance unit expanded its use of Chainlink's Proof of Reserve standard to publish on-chain reserve data for nxtAssets' Bitcoin Core exchange-traded product. Chainlink announced the integration on 19 August 2026, with data published on the Arbitrum network.
