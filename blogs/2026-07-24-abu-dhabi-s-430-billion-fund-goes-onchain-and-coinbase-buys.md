Mubadala Capital, which manages about $430 billion, launched a tokenized version of a private markets fund on 23 July 2026 using KAIO infrastructure. The fund runs on Base, Solana and Sui, has drawn roughly $75 million, and Coinbase took exposure on its balance sheet.
