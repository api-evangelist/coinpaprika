Evercrest Technologies, the developer behind KelpDAO, filed a civil claim in the Supreme Court of British Columbia against LayerZero Labs and co-founder Bryan Pellegrino. The suit ties April's $292 million rsETH bridge exploit to LayerZero's own security setup.
